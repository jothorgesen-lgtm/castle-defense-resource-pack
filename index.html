Castle Defense Red Moon

On Blood Moon nights the sky over the castle turns red. Add this pack and the moon turns red with it. It also carves the boss statues in the Great Hall in gray stone; without it they’re plain stone blocks. And it makes the castle’s pink concrete powder look like dark reinforced deepslate, and turns all acacia wood into black ebony, with solid paneled doors and trapdoors. Ender chests become iron-bound strongboxes. The Forge becomes a full blacksmith’s corner with a glowing hearth, the Kennel gets its own model, and the War Room table shows little 3D game pieces for each event night. Five colors of concrete powder become solid iron gate blocks, one look for each gate upgrade: magenta, lime, light blue, yellow and purple. In English, they’re named to match: Ebony Planks, Strongbox and so on.

Download Castle-Defense-Resource-Pack.zip

Version DUAIP · Updated Nov 21, 2026

  1. Save the zip. Don’t unzip it. If your Mac unzips it anyway, use the folder it made instead; that works too.

  2. In Minecraft, open Options → Resource Packs → Open Pack Folder and drag the zip into that folder.

  3. Back in Minecraft, click the arrow on Castle-Defense-Resource-Pack to move it to Selected, then click Done.

You only do this once. The pack repaints the full moon, which Castle Defense only shows on Blood Moon nights, gives the boss statues their gray stone look, turns pink concrete powder into reinforced deepslate and five other concrete powder colors into iron gate blocks, and turns acacia wood into ebony. It stays on in your other worlds too, so their full moons turn red, their concrete powder in those colors changes too and their acacia looks like ebony as well; move it back to Available to switch it off. Already have the older Castle-Defense-Blood-Moon pack? This one replaces it, so move the old one back to Available. To check you have the newest, look under the pack’s name in the Resource Packs list: its version should match the one above. Made for Minecraft Java 26.3.
